The Reverse Accessibility Lab

A challenge by Genio

Everything broken here is broken on purpose.

The rooms deliberately break accessibility guidelines so you can feel the frustration for yourself. They’re a simulation, not a mistake. See every issue we’ve built in, and why (opens in a new tab).

No prior experience with assistive technology needed.

Each room explains what it simulates and gives you a hint. Try to feel the friction first — and if you get stuck, every room has an “I can’t work this out” button that shows you how.
