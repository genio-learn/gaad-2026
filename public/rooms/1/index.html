The Dead Mouse

Your pointer device has stopped working. The panel is locked. Your clue is inside — but the mouse won't get you there.

What this room simulates

Using a computer without a mouse. Many students can’t use a mouse or trackpad — because of a physical disability, a tremor, repetitive strain injury, or because they’re blind and navigate with a screen reader. They rely on the keyboard, or on switch devices that work like one.

We’ve switched the mouse off in this room on purpose so you can try it for yourself.

Hint: When a mouse won't work, there's always another way in. Try pressing Tab to move around the page.
I can’t work this out — show me how

How to solve it

  1. Press Tab. A glowing outline shows which part of the page you’re on.
  2. Keep pressing Tab until the Unlock Panel button is outlined. (Shift+Tab moves backwards.)
  3. Press Enter to open the panel and reveal your clue.

On a phone or tablet you’ll need a connected keyboard for this room.

Why this matters for students

A student who can’t use a mouse depends on every link, button and menu being reachable with Tab and usable with Enter or Space — and on a clear outline showing where they are. When a website or app misses either one, the student is simply stuck, often with no workaround.
